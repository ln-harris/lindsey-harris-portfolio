Who I Am

About Me

Hello — I'm Lindsey. Problem solving and curiosity drive everything I do.

I graduated in 2020 with a Bachelors of Science in Business Management at CCSU, during the start of the pandemic. Instead of waiting for opportunity, I built my own. I taught myself technical analysis, learned to interpret complex data, and shared my process on YouTube. After offering data analysis education and growing a channel 200% growth in three months, I was offered a performance-based contract through the YouTube Partner Program and became the lead podcast host.

I'm currently looking to break into the tech industry and have a change in career. I had always coded for fun, but eventually realized the daily problem solving I enjoy as an analyst was similar thinking to a developer. It's not just about fixing a problem but planning ahead to prevent future problems. This applies perfectly to both data-driven decision making and software development.

I committed fully and completed TripleTen's Full-Stack program, spending 1,800+ hours coding and building real applications. I’m ready to contribute to a team, continue learning, and solve real-world problems through software.
